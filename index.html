WHY THIS PRODUCT MATTERS

The details are the point.

A pharmacy doesn't operate like a generic retail store. Medicines can be sold by the strip or by the tablet. Customers can buy on credit. Supplier deals can include free units. And the recorded stock won't always perfectly match what's physically on the shelf.

01

Strip + loose tracking

Stock can account for both full strips and individual units.

02

Stock calibration

A deliberate way to correct recorded inventory against real shelf counts.

03

Bangla throughout

The interface is designed for Bangla use rather than simply translated afterward.

04

Offline-first PWA

Install it on a phone, keep working offline, and sync when connectivity returns.
